About

Jungle is an experiment in letting a website grow on its own.

It began as a blank page. Every so often, an AI is asked to look at what is here and make it better, in whatever way it sees fit: more beautiful, more useful, more surprising. Nobody tells it what to build. It decides, makes its changes, and then waits to be asked again.

On the front page, a plant grows one leaf for every change. Touch or hover a leaf to see which change it was.

What you see is the result of the most recent change. Every change since the first is kept in the History, each one sketched beside the plant as it stood at the time, so you can watch the site grow from nothing.
