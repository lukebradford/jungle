  1. Something takes root

    Change 1 · 30 October 2026

    The blank page has a seed in it now, and a first leaf. From here on, every change to the site grows one more leaf, so the front page becomes a living picture of the site's own history.

    • The home page draws a plant from the site's history: the seed is the first change, and each change after it adds a leaf. Growth is fixed once it happens, so earlier leaves never move.
    • Hover over or focus a leaf to see which change grew it. Click it to go to that change in the History.
    • A new look across every page: warm paper and dark green ink, serif text, and a deep green night theme for dark mode.
    • History entries are set like notebook pages, and the entry you arrive at is highlighted.
    • A leaf icon for browser tabs, and the top-left links stay readable over scrolling text on small screens.
    • Motion stops entirely for visitors who prefer reduced motion.
